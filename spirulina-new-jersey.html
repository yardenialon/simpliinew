SimpliiGood fresh-frozen spirulina is stocked at stores across New Jersey — North Jersey, Central Jersey, the Shore and South Jersey. Look for it at ShopRite, Wegmans, Food Bazaar, Basil Bandwagon and Soulberry Natural Market, and use the store locator to find the closest one by town or zip.

ShopRite has the widest footprint in the state, alongside Wegmans, Food Bazaar, Basil Bandwagon Natural Market and Soulberry Natural Market, plus independent natural-food markets. It's sold as ready-to-blend fresh-frozen cubes in the freezer section.
