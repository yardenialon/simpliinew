Where can I buy fresh spirulina in New York?

SimpliiGood fresh-frozen spirulina is sold at 160+ stores across New York State — more than any other state. That includes 70+ stores across the five boroughs, plus Long Island, Westchester, the Hudson Valley and upstate cities like Rochester, Buffalo and Syracuse. Use the store locator to find the closest one, or order online for delivery.

Is SimpliiGood spirulina available in NYC?

Yes — at 70+ stores across New York City: roughly 40+ in Brooklyn, 25+ in Manhattan, and more in Queens and the Bronx, at retailers like Food Bazaar, Key Food, Westside Market, Citarella and Fairway.

Which supermarkets sell SimpliiGood spirulina in New York?

Across New York you'll find it at Wegmans, Food Bazaar, ShopRite, Citarella, DeCicco & Sons, Key Food, Super Fresh, Foodtown, Fairway Market, Westside Market, Gourmet Garage, North Shore Farms and many independent natural-food markets.

Can I get fresh spirulina delivered in New York?

Yes. If you'd rather not visit a store, order SimpliiGood online at shop.simpliigood.com and have it shipped frozen to your door anywhere in New York.

Is SimpliiGood a New York company?

SimpliiGood runs its US operation out of New York, and New York is the brand's largest market — more stores here carry fresh-frozen spirulina than in any other state.
