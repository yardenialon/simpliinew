The basics

What is fresh-frozen
spirulina?

Fresh-frozen spirulina is live spirulina, flash-frozen within 24 hours of harvest into ready-to-blend cubes — instead of being heat-dried into powder. One ingredient: spirulina and water. Because it's frozen rather than dried, it stays virtually flavor-free and keeps its heat-sensitive compounds intact. Here's exactly what that means.

Pool to freezer

How it's made.

1

Grown fresh

Spirulina is grown in greenhouse pools of food-grade fresh water and harvested daily — alive and vivid green.

2

Flash-frozen in 24h

Within 24 hours of harvest the fresh biomass is flash-frozen — no heat, no drying, no powdering.

3

Portioned into cubes

It's frozen into pre-measured cubes you drop straight into a blender. One ingredient, zero mess.

That 24-hour window is the whole idea. Most spirulina on the shelf is dried — heated and milled into a shelf-stable powder. Drying is what gives spirulina its swampy, fishy reputation and costs some of its most delicate compounds. Freezing fresh spirulina, the way you'd freeze fruit, skips all of that.

Frozen vs. dried

Why frozen, not powder.

Taste. Fresh-frozen spirulina is virtually flavor-free — a cube disappears into a fruit smoothie. Dried powder tastes distinctly fishy and takes the whole glass over.

Heat-sensitive nutrients. Phycocyanin (the blue antioxidant) and chlorophyll are sensitive to the heat used in drying. Freezing keeps them intact and the color vivid.

Convenience. No measuring, no clumping, no green dust — just a pre-portioned cube. The one trade-off: cubes live in the freezer, so powder still wins for travel. See the complete breakdown in fresh vs. powder spirulina.

What's in it

One ingredient, real nutrition.

A 2-cube serving (28 g) delivers 2 g of complete plant protein, 10% of the Daily Value for iron and vitamin C — for just 10 calories, with no fat and no sugar. See what's inside spirulina in full, or dig into spirulina and iron. Then drop a cube into one of our recipes.

Common questions

Fresh-frozen spirulina, answered.

What is fresh-frozen spirulina?

Spirulina harvested fresh and flash-frozen within 24 hours — instead of heat-dried into powder — and portioned into ready-to-blend cubes. SimpliiGood cubes are one ingredient: spirulina and water. Freezing keeps them virtually flavor-free and preserves heat-sensitive compounds like phycocyanin and chlorophyll.

Is frozen spirulina better than powder?

For taste and heat-sensitive nutrients, yes — drying concentrates the fishy flavor and degrades phycocyanin and chlorophyll, while freezing avoids both. Powder still wins on shelf life and portability. See the full fresh vs. powder comparison.

How is spirulina frozen?

SimpliiGood grows spirulina in greenhouse pools of fresh water, harvests daily, and flash-freezes the fresh biomass within 24 hours into pre-portioned cubes — no drying, no powdering. From pool to freezer, like frozen fruit.

Does frozen spirulina taste fishy?

No. The fishy taste comes from drying spirulina into powder. Fresh-frozen spirulina is virtually flavor-free, so a cube disappears into a fruit smoothie.
