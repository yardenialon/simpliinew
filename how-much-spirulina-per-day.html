How to use

How much spirulina
per day?

The honest answer: spirulina is a food, not a measured supplement, so there's no single required dose. For most people a 2-cube serving (28 g) a day is a great everyday amount. Here's how to think about the right amount for you.

Most people: 2 cubes a day.

A 2-cube serving (28 g) blended into one smoothie adds about 2 g of complete plant protein, 10% of the Daily Value for iron and a dose of antioxidants — for roughly 10 calories, with no fishy taste. Simple, repeatable, done.

Match it to your goal.

Everyday nutrition: start with 2 cubes in a daily smoothie. It's enough to feel like a habit without thinking about it.

Leaning on it for iron or protein: 2 cubes covers 10% DV iron and 2 g protein; step up to 3–4 cubes for a bigger contribution. For iron, blend with vitamin-C-rich fruit to absorb more — see spirulina & iron.

New to spirulina: start with 1 cube for a few days, then move to 2. There's no loading phase — it's food, not medicine.

Common questions

How much spirulina, answered.

How much spirulina should you take per day?

There's no single required dose — spirulina is a food. A common everyday amount is a 2-cube serving (28 g): about 2 g complete protein, 10% DV iron and antioxidants in one smoothie, for ~10 calories. More adds more nutrition; no need to megadose. Follow your provider's guidance for a specific goal.

How many SimpliiGood cubes should I take a day?

A standard serving is 2 cubes (28 g) a day, dropped straight into a smoothie. One cube is a lighter addition; 3–4 cubes is a bigger boost. It's a whole food, so there's no fixed rule.

Can you take too much spirulina?

For most healthy adults, everyday amounts of quality spirulina are well tolerated, and there's no need to overdo it. Choose a Non-GMO Verified source grown in food-grade conditions. If you're pregnant, nursing, on medication or have PKU, ask your doctor about the right amount.

When is the best time to take spirulina?

Any time that fits your routine — often a morning or post-workout smoothie. For iron, pair it with vitamin C (blend with orange or berries) and keep it a little apart from tea or coffee.

Is it OK to take spirulina every day?

For most healthy adults, a daily serving is a fine part of a balanced diet — exactly how fresh-frozen cubes are designed to be used. Check with your doctor first if you're pregnant, nursing, on medication or managing a condition.
